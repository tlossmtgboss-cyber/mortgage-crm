Minimum Credit Score

All Agency Comparison Chart with Investor Overlays

Although the agencies publish their minimum credit score as low as 500 (FHA) or 620 (Fannie), it is best practice to check with the lender or investor to ensure that you also meet their requirements, which are often different (and higher) than the agency minimum.

As of April 22, 2024, Freddie Mac issued bulletin 2024-3 announcing new Credit Score Models, and Fannie Mae also announced new models with a Casefile Score of 612 and FHA Score of 615, which are for both purchase and streamline refinances. The standard FICO model remains in effect for lenders not yet in the pilot programs. Check with your lender or investor to determine which credit score model is used.

Agency Guidelines
Fannie Mae
B3-5.1-01
DU Guidelines

Minimum credit score requirement for manually underwritten loans is 620 for fixed-rate, and 640 for ARMs.

For DU recommendations, the minimum representative credit score is 620. For HomeReady mortgages, 620 applies with ≤ 97% LTV for fixed-rate loans.

  • Desktop Underwriter (DU): 620
  • The reference score for > 1 score on borrower: middle score
  • Multiple borrowers: lowest of all representative scores
  • HomeReady: 620, up to 97% LTV (fixed only)
DU Validation Service & Desktop Originator:
  • The minimum credit score is 620 when DU finds an "Approve" or "Approve/Ineligible" recommendation
  • Tradeline verification is automated through Fannie Mae's partnership with credit providers
  • If an "Approve/Ineligible" is received, the loan may be resubmitted once conditions are cleared to see if it converts to "Approve"
Freddie Mac
Guide Section 5201
LP Guidelines

Freddie Mac sets the minimum credit score for its purchase programs. The minimum indicator score is 620 for Loan Prospector (LP). Manual underwriting and Freddie Mac also require 620.

  • The Mortgage Indicator Score is the middle of 3 scores or lower of 2
  • The Minimum Mortgage Indicator score for an LP Primary Residence: 620
  • For Mortgages on investment properties requiring manual underwriting: 620
  • For Mortgage secured by a Manufactured Home: 620

Additional Freddie Mac credit considerations:

  • If neither borrower has a credit score, manual underwriting is required (traditional credit evaluation or alternative credit)
  • A Borrower must not have had a delinquent, or deficient (in terms of a short sale or deed-in-lieu) Freddie Mac Mortgage, or have been foreclosed upon by Freddie Mac
  • Freddie Mac recommends the lender check the Borrower's credit report to confirm the Mortgage the Borrower is seeking to refinance is current (at least 12 months on-time payments required for no-cash-out)
FHA
4000.1 II.A
TOTAL Scorecard

The Borrower is not eligible if the FHA required Minimum Decision Credit Score (MDCS) is less than 500.

  • Credit 580+: up to 96.5% LTV for purchases, eligible for maximum financing
  • Credit 500-579: maximum 90% LTV for purchase
  • If borrower has no FICO: must be manually underwritten using non-traditional credit

VA
26-7 Ch.4

Although VA establishes no minimum FICO credit score requirement, most lenders set a minimum of 620. Lenders evaluate the Borrower's overall credit history with particular attention to the most recent 12 months.

  • VA strongly encourages lenders to use a 620 FICO as a guideline
  • If a lender denies a VA loan below 620, the applicant has no recourse — this is a lender overlay, not a VA requirement
USDA
USDA HB-1-3555

GUS (Guaranteed Underwriting System) requires a minimum FICO of 640 for automated approval. Borrowers below 640 may be manually underwritten. USDA sets no absolute minimum, but the automated system has a floor.

  • GUS Accept with 640+: streamlined documentation
  • Below 640: manual underwriting with alternative credit history required
Company Overlay
CMG v3.2 §2.1

Company minimum across all programs:

  • Conventional (Fannie/Freddie): 640 (overlay above agency 620)
  • FHA: 640 (overlay above agency 580)
  • VA: 620 (matches most lender practice)
  • USDA: 640 (matches GUS automated floor)
  • Non-QM Bank Statement: 660
  • Non-QM DSCR: 680
