2. Questionable Decisions

Scope

160+ AI Agent Tools

The LangGraph orchestrator has 160 registered tools. Even with intent-based tool loading (8-16 per request), this is an enormous surface area to maintain. Tools use parameterized SQL queries (not string interpolation). Most competitors achieve their AI features with 10-20 well-tested tools.

Strategy

Feature Breadth — Build-vs-Buy Tradeoff

The codebase includes natively built: video meetings (WebRTC + SFU), power dialer, content marketing, carousel builder, scheduling, IT helpdesk, voicemail drops, live call whisper, and more. This is a deliberate strategy to eliminate third-party subscription costs ($2,000-5,000+/mo across Zoom, Calendly, Freshdesk, etc.). The risk isn’t that these exist — it’s that many are still half-integrated. The priority should be hardening each built module to production reliability before adding the next one.

Infrastructure

Railway for Production

Railway has a ~20 connection DB limit and 500 logs/sec rate limit. You’ve already hit connection exhaustion issues. An enterprise mortgage platform needs production-grade infrastructure (AWS RDS, proper connection pooling, separate read replicas).

Key Risk

The Hardening Gap

The build-vs-buy strategy is sound — owning the stack eliminates subscription costs, removes integration headaches, and gives you full control over the UX. The risk is that half-built replacements are worse than paid subscriptions. A video meeting that drops calls is worse than paying for Zoom.
