# Your Document Portal -- A Guide for Borrowers

---

## Welcome to Your Document Portal

This portal is where you send us the documents we need for your mortgage. You can also sign papers, check your loan progress, and message your loan team -- all in one place.

**How to get in:**
Your loan officer will send you an email with a link to your portal. You can also use an access code they give you. Either way gets you to the same place.

**Your information is safe.**
Your portal is protected by bank-level security. Only you and your loan team can see your documents. All files are encrypted when they are sent and when they are stored. We never share your information with anyone who is not part of your loan.

## Logging In

### Using Your Email Link

1. Check your email for a message from your loan team.
2. Click the link in the email. It will say something like "Access Your Document Portal."
3. The link takes you straight into your portal. No password needed.

Your link is good for 24 hours. After that, you will need a new one.

### Using Your Access Code

1. Go to the portal sign-in page. Your loan officer will give you the web address.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Enter the 6-digit access code your loan officer gave you.
4. Click "Sign In."

### My Link Expired

Links last 24 hours. If yours has expired:

- Ask your loan officer to send you a new link. You can call or email them.
- You can also use your access code instead (see above).

### I Forgot My Access Code

- Contact your loan officer. They can send you a new code or a new email link.
- You can reach them at the contact information shown on your portal sign-in page.

## Your Document Checklist

When you log in, you will see a checklist of documents we need from you. Each item shows what we need, why we need it, and its current status.

### Understanding What Is Needed

Your checklist is grouped into categories:

- **Income** -- Documents that show how much you earn.
- **Assets** -- Documents that show your savings and investments.
- **Identity** -- Documents that prove who you are.
- **Property** -- Documents related to the home you are buying or refinancing.
- **Other** -- Any extra items your loan officer has asked for.

### Document Status Icons

Each item on your checklist has a status:

| Status | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Outstanding** | We still need this document from you. You have not sent it yet. |
| **Under Review** | You sent it and we are looking at it now. Nothing else to do -- just wait. |
| **Approved** | We have reviewed it and it looks good. This one is done. |
| **Needs Resubmission** | There is a problem with what you sent. We will tell you what is wrong so you can send it again. |
| **Waived** | Your loan officer decided this document is not needed after all. You can skip it. |

### Priority Indicators

Some documents are more urgent than others:

- **Critical** -- We need this right away. Your loan cannot move forward without it.
- **High** -- Please send this soon. It is important.
- **Normal** -- Send this when you can. There is no rush yet.
- **Low** -- This may be needed later. You can wait on this one.

### Due Dates

Some items have a due date. This is the date we need the document by. Try to send documents before their due date so your loan stays on track.

If a document is past due, it will be marked so you know to send it as soon as possible.

## Uploading Documents

### How to Upload from Your Computer

1. Find the item on your checklist and click "Upload."
2. A window will open. Find the file on your computer and select it.
3. Click "Open" or "Upload."
4. You will see a message that your file was received.

You can also drag a file from your computer and drop it right onto the upload area.

### How to Upload from Your Phone

1. Open your portal in your phone's web browser.
2. Tap "Upload" next to the item on your checklist.
3. Choose "Take Photo" to use your camera, or "Photo Library" to pick a file you already have.
4. If you take a photo, snap the picture and tap "Use Photo."
5. Your file will upload right away.

### Accepted File Types

You can upload these types of files:

- **PDF** -- Best for most documents.
- **JPG / JPEG** -- Photos and scanned images.
- **PNG** -- Photos and scanned images.
- **TIFF** -- Scanned documents.
- **HEIC** -- Photos from iPhones.

### Maximum File Size

Each file can be up to **25 MB**. Most documents are well under this limit.

If your file is too big, try scanning at a lower resolution or taking a new photo.

### Tips for Good Quality Uploads

Good uploads save time. A blurry or cut-off document will be sent back to you, which slows things down.

- **Use good lighting.** Avoid shadows on the document. Natural light works best.
- **Capture the entire document.** All four edges should be visible. Do not cut off any text.
- **Make sure text is readable.** If you cannot read it on your screen, we cannot read it either.
- **Keep the document flat.** Lay it on a flat surface before taking a photo.
- **Upload all pages.** If a document has multiple pages (like a tax return or bank statement), upload every page. You can upload them as one multi-page PDF or as separate files.
- **Do not send screenshots.** Screenshots of bank apps or websites will be rejected. We need the official document or statement.

### What Happens After You Upload

1. Your file is scanned for safety and quality.
2. Our system automatically figures out what type of document it is.
3. The item on your checklist changes to "Under Review."
4. Your loan team reviews the document. This usually takes 1-2 business days.
5. If everything looks good, the status changes to "Approved."
6. If there is a problem, the status changes to "Needs Resubmission" and you will see a note explaining what to fix.

## Common Documents Explained

Here is a quick guide to the documents borrowers are most often asked to provide.

### Pay Stubs

**What:** Your most recent pay stubs covering the last 30 days.
**Where to find them:** From your employer's payroll system, your HR department, or your online payroll portal.
**Tips:** Make sure your name, employer name, pay period dates, and year-to-date earnings are all showing.

### W-2s

**What:** Your W-2 forms from the last 2 years.
**Where to find them:** From your employer each January, or from the IRS website (irs.gov).
**Tips:** Upload all pages. If you had more than one job, we need W-2s from each employer.

### Tax Returns

**What:** Your federal tax returns from the last 2 years, all pages and all schedules.
**Where to find them:** From your tax preparer, your tax software (TurboTax, H&R Block, etc.), or the IRS.
**Tips:** We need every page, including schedules and attachments. Do not skip any pages even if they look blank.

### Bank Statements

**What:** Your most recent 2 months of bank statements for all accounts.
**Where to find them:** Download from your bank's website or app. Look for "Statements" or "Documents."
**Tips:** We need all pages of each statement, including pages that say "This page intentionally left blank." Do not send screenshots of your balance -- we need the full official statement.

### Driver's License

**What:** A photo of your valid driver's license, front and back.
**Where to find them:** Take a photo with your phone.
**Tips:** Make sure the photo is clear and all text is readable. Both sides are required.

### Gift Letter

**What:** A signed letter from the person giving you money toward your home purchase.
**Where to find them:** Your loan officer can give you a template to fill out.
**Tips:** The letter must state the amount, that it is a gift (not a loan), the donor's name, and their relationship to you. The donor will also need to show where the funds came from.

### Letters of Explanation

**What:** A written statement from you explaining something in your credit or financial history.
**When needed:** Your loan officer will ask for one if there is something in your file that needs more context, such as a gap in employment, a large deposit, or a late payment.
**Tips:** Keep it short and honest. Your loan officer will tell you exactly what to address. Sign and date the letter.

## Signing Documents Electronically

Some documents need your signature. You can sign them right in the portal without printing anything.

### How E-Signing Works

When a document is ready for your signature, you will see it in the "Signatures" section of your portal. You may also get an email letting you know a document is waiting.

### Your Consent to Electronic Signatures

Before you sign your first document, the portal will show you a consent notice. This explains:

- You agree to sign and receive documents electronically.
- You can withdraw your consent at any time.
- You have the right to receive paper copies instead.
- You need a modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge) to view documents.

You must click "I Agree" before you can sign electronically. This consent is required by federal law (the ESIGN Act).

### Step-by-Step Signing Process

1. Go to the "Signatures" section of your portal, or click the link in the email you received.
2. If this is your first time, read and accept the electronic consent notice.
3. If an access code is required, enter the code your loan officer gave you.
4. Review the document carefully. Read every page.
5. Click or tap each signature field where your signature is needed. The fields are highlighted for you.
6. Type your name or draw your signature when prompted.
7. Fill in any other required fields (date, initials, etc.).
8. Click "Submit" when you are done.
9. You will see a confirmation that your signature was recorded.
10. A signed copy is saved in your portal for your records.

### What the Access Code Is For

For some documents, your loan officer may set up an access code as an extra layer of security. This makes sure that only you can sign the document. You enter the code before signing. Your loan officer will give you the code separately.

### How to Decline to Sign Electronically

You have every right to decline. If you prefer not to sign electronically:

1. Let your loan officer know that you would like paper copies.
2. Your loan team will print the documents and arrange for you to sign in person or by mail.
3. This may add a few extra days to your loan timeline.

### Requesting a Paper Copy

Even after signing electronically, you can request a paper copy of any document you signed. Contact your loan officer and they will arrange it for you.

## Tracking Your Progress

Your portal shows you where your loan stands in the process. Look for the progress tracker at the top of your portal.

### What Each Stage Means

| Stage | What Is Happening |
|---|---|
| **Application** | We have your application and are getting started. |
| **Processing** | We are gathering and organizing all your documents and information. |
| **Submitted to Underwriting** | Your file has been sent to the underwriter for review. |
| **Under Review** | The underwriter is reviewing your file. |
| **Approved** | Great news -- your loan has been approved. There may be a few final items to take care of. |
| **Clear to Close** | Everything is ready. We are preparing your closing documents. |
| **Closing** | Your closing documents are being finalized. Your closing date is coming up soon. |
| **Funded** | Your loan has been funded. Congratulations! |

### Estimated Timeline

Your portal may show an estimated closing date. This is our best estimate based on where your loan is today. It can change if we are waiting on documents or if something needs extra review.

The fastest way to keep your loan on schedule is to send documents quickly and respond to any messages from your loan team.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### "Why do you need so many documents?"

Federal rules require us to verify your income, assets, and identity before approving a mortgage. We know it feels like a lot, but every document has a purpose and helps us get your loan to the finish line.

### "How long does the review take?"

Most documents are reviewed within 1-2 business days. If there is a problem, we will let you know right away so you can fix it.

### "What if I don't have a document you need?"

Tell your loan officer. There may be an alternative document we can accept, or they may be able to waive the requirement. Do not wait in silence -- let us know and we will work it out together.

### "Can I upload documents from my phone?"

Yes. The portal works on phones and tablets. You can take a photo of a document with your camera and upload it right away. See the "Uploading Documents" section for tips on getting a good photo.

### "Is my information secure?"

Yes. Your documents are encrypted in transit and at rest. Only your loan team can access them. We use the same level of security that banks use. Your portal link is unique to you and cannot be shared.

### "Who can see my documents?"

Only the people working on your loan can see your documents. This includes your loan officer, your loan processor, and the underwriter assigned to your file. No one outside your loan team has access.

### "What if I uploaded the wrong document?"

It happens. Simply upload the correct document. Your loan team will see the new file during their review. If you want to let them know, send a message through the portal saying which item you re-uploaded.

### "Can someone else upload documents for me?"

Your portal link is tied to your loan and your email address. For security, we recommend that only you use your portal. If someone else needs to help (like a spouse or co-borrower), they can receive their own portal link from your loan officer.
