# Perennia AI UVIP Quick Start Guide

## Welcome to Perennia AI UVIP!

This guide will get you up and running in under 10 minutes.

---

## Step 1: Log In

1. Go to: **https://mortgage-crm-production-7a9a.up.railway.app**
2. Enter your email and temporary password
3. You'll be prompted to set a new password
4. Complete the onboarding wizard (takes ~2 minutes)

---

## Step 2: Record Your First Call

### Automatic Recording
All calls through the built-in dialer are automatically recorded and transcribed.

1. Click **"Dialer"** in the left navigation
2. Enter a phone number or select a contact
3. Click **"Call"**
4. After the call, view the transcript and AI analysis

### Manual Recording Upload
1. Click **"Calls"** in the left navigation
2. Click **"Upload Recording"**
3. Select your audio file (MP3, WAV, M4A)
4. Wait for transcription (usually under 2 minutes)

---

## Step 3: Create Your First Video Clip

Video clips are personalized video messages you can send to borrowers.

1. Click **"Clips"** in the left navigation
2. Click **"Create Clip"**
3. Choose a template:
   - **Rate Lock Explanation** - Explain current rates
   - **Application Update** - Status update for borrower
   - **Document Request** - Request missing documents
   - **Closing Prep** - Prepare for closing day
4. Click **"Record"** and allow camera/microphone access
5. Record your message (keep it under 2 minutes)
6. Click **"Done"** → Preview → **"Save"**

### Sharing Your Clip
1. Click the clip you want to share
2. Click **"Share"**
3. Enter recipient's email or copy the link
4. Recipient can view without logging in

---

## Step 4: View Your Analytics

### Pipeline Dashboard
- See all your loans at a glance
- Filter by stage, LO, or date range
- Track conversion rates

### Performance Analytics
- Call volume and duration
- Clip engagement rates
- AI coaching scores

### Team Dashboard (Managers)
- Compare team member performance
- Identify coaching opportunities
- Track team metrics

---

## Step 5: Use the AI Assistant

The AI assistant can help you with:
- **"What are my priorities today?"** - Get your daily task list
- **"Show me loans closing this week"** - Pipeline queries
- **"Should I lock this rate?"** - Rate lock advice
- **"Draft an email to [borrower]"** - Communication help

1. Click the **AI chat** button (bottom right)
2. Type your question naturally
3. Get instant, context-aware responses

---

## Pro Tips

### Get the Most from Video Clips
- Keep clips under 90 seconds
- Look at the camera, not the screen
- Mention the borrower's name
- End with a clear call-to-action

### Boost Your AI Coaching Score
- Speak clearly and at moderate pace
- Use positive language
- Listen more than you talk
- Address concerns directly

### Pipeline Management
- Update loan stages in real-time
- Add notes after every call
- Set follow-up tasks immediately
