Interactive Morning Briefing

Aria sends your daily priorities. You reply with what to handle. AI executes and reports back. All via email.

Approved 2026-05-07 Tim Loss + Claude

The Conversation Loop

1
Briefing
Aria → LO • Daily at briefing_hour
Prioritized action-item email with numbered tasks, context, and suggested actions. Sent from aria@perenniaai.com.
↓
2
LO Reply
LO → Aria • Natural language
LO replies to the email with instructions. System polls inbox every 60s, matches reply to thread.
↓
3
Confirmation
Aria → LO • Structured task list
AI parses the reply, sends back a specific plan. "Here's what I'll do: [list]. Reply 'approved' to proceed."
↓
4
Approval
LO → Aria • Approve / modify / cancel
LO approves or requests changes. If changes, loops back to Step 3. If approved, execution begins.
↓
5
Results
Aria → LO • Single summary
One email after all tasks complete: what succeeded, what failed, what needs manual attention.

Briefing Priority Order

Pipeline-first. Items ordered by urgency and impact on active deals.

  1. At-risk loans needing action today
    Days stalled, missing docs, compliance issues
  2. Expiring rate locks
    Within lock_expiring_days threshold
  3. Conditions & docs due
    Outstanding underwriting conditions, pending documents
  4. Stale leads to follow up
    No contact in stale_lead_days
  5. Today's appointments
    Calendar items with borrower context
  6. New leads to contact
    Unworked inbound leads

Thread State Machine

BRIEFING_SENT → AWAITING_REPLY → PARSING_INSTRUCTIONS → CONFIRMATION_SENT → AWAITING_APPROVAL → EXECUTING → RESULTS_SENT
Loops: AWAITING_APPROVAL → CONFIRMATION_SENT (LO requests changes)
Terminal states: RESULTS_SENT, EXPIRED (no reply in 4h), CANCELLED (LO cancels), FAILED (unrecoverable error)

Reply Matching

When a reply arrives at aria@perenniaai.com, the system matches it to a briefing thread using three strategies in priority order:

  1. In-Reply-To / References headers
    Standard email threading. Match outbound_message_id to the original briefing email.
  2. X-Perennia-Thread-Id header
    Custom header with UUID thread token. Preserved by most email clients on reply.
  3. Sender email + briefing date
    Last resort fallback. Match sender to user, find most recent active thread from today.
