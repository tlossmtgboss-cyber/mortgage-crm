# Call Intelligence Training Guide

## What is Call Intelligence?

Call Intelligence is Perennia AI's automated call analysis system. Every borrower call you make or receive is automatically transcribed, analyzed, scored, and turned into actionable data — without you lifting a finger.

Think of it as having a junior loan officer, an underwriter, and a compliance officer all listening to every call simultaneously, taking perfect notes, and filing everything into the right place.

---

## How It Works: The Pipeline

When a call ends, here's what happens behind the scenes:

### Step 1: Recording Capture
Your calls are captured through whichever channel you use:
- **Inbound calls** via the AI Receptionist (Aria)
- **Outbound calls** from the built-in dialer
- **Click-to-call** from any lead or loan profile
- **Uploaded recordings** from external calls

### Step 2: Transcription (Automatic)
Within seconds of a call ending, the recording is sent to our transcription engine:
- **Speaker diarization** — the system knows who said what (you vs. the borrower)
- **Word-level timestamps** — every word is time-stamped for searchability
- **Confidence scoring** — the system flags sections where audio quality was poor
- **Provider**: Deepgram Nova-2 (primary), with automatic fallback providers

### Step 3: AI Extraction (6 Domains)
A single optimized AI call analyzes the full transcript and extracts structured data across six mortgage domains:

| Domain | What It Extracts |
|--------|-----------------|
| **Identity** | Borrower name, DOB, last 4 of SSN, contact info, email, phone |
| **Property** | Subject property address, purchase price, occupancy type, property details |
| **Employment** | Employer name, job title, years employed, income type |
| **Financial** | Income sources, assets, liabilities, down payment, reserves |
| **Compliance** | Declarations (bankruptcy, litigation, etc.), required disclosures |
| **Intent** | Loan purpose, timeline, co-borrower info, preferences |

Each extracted field includes a **confidence score** (0-100%). High-confidence extractions (85%+) are auto-approved. Low-confidence extractions are sent to your review queue.

> **PII Security**: Full SSNs are never stored. Only the last 4 digits are captured. All PII is redacted before being sent to the AI for analysis.

### Step 4: Post-Call Automation
After extraction, five automations run simultaneously:

1. **AI Summary** — A concise summary of the call (who, what, decisions made, next steps) is generated and attached to the loan file. Summaries come in four flavors:
   - *Quick* — 5 bullet points, 30-second read
   - *Standard* — 2-minute full analysis
   - *Executive* — Manager-focused with conversion probability
   - *Coaching* — Training-focused with skill improvement areas

2. **Action Items** — Tasks mentioned during the call are automatically extracted and created in your task list. Each task includes a title, description, priority, and due date.

3. **Follow-up SMS Draft** — A personalized follow-up text message is drafted based on the call content. Review and send with one tap.

4. **Compliance Score** — Every call is scored on seven compliance dimensions:
   - TRID Disclosure (25%)
   - Fair Lending (20%)
   - Consent Obtained (15%)
   - Rate Accuracy (15%)
   - Identity Verification (10%)
   - Fee Transparency (10%)
   - DNC Compliance (5%)

5. **Sentiment Analysis** — The system tracks borrower sentiment throughout the call, identifying positive and negative moments, overall trajectory, and risk flags.

### Step 5: Three AI Agents Analyze in Parallel

Once the transcript is ready, three specialized AI agents process the call simultaneously:

**The Scribe** — Creates a comprehensive call recap including:
- 5 C's of Credit analysis (credit, capacity, collateral, character, cash)
- Key decisions and commitments made
- Next steps for both parties

**The Junior Loan Officer** — Runs application-level analysis:
- Pre-qualification calculation
- Debt-to-income (DTI) analysis
- Document requirements identified
- Loan product recommendations
- Risk identification

**The Underwriter** — Performs risk assessment:
- Underwriting bullet points
- Risk flags by category (credit, income, employment, property, compliance, assets, DTI)
- Severity ratings (low/medium/high/critical)
- Specific file concerns to address

All artifacts generated by these agents appear in your review queue for approval before being applied.

### Step 6: Quality Assurance Scoring

Every call is automatically scored on 10 criteria:

| Criteria | Weight | What It Measures |
|----------|--------|-----------------|
| Greeting Quality | 10% | Professional welcome, appropriate length |
| Active Listening | 12% | Acknowledgments, clarifying questions |
| Problem Resolution | 15% | Call outcome effectiveness |
| Product Knowledge | 12% | Mortgage terminology accuracy |
| Communication Clarity | 12% | Message structure and organization |
| Empathy & Rapport | 10% | Personalization, warmth |
| Compliance Adherence | 12% | Required disclosures, no guarantees |
| Call Control | 7% | Duration optimization, guidance |
| Closing Technique | 5% | Proper close with clear CTA |
| Professionalism | 5% | Consistent tone, no slang |

**Score Range**: 1.0 - 5.0
- **Excellent**: 4.5+
- **Good**: 4.0 - 4.5
- **Acceptable**: 3.5 - 4.0
- **Needs Improvement**: 3.0 - 3.5
- **Critical**: Below 2.5

## Live Call Whisper (Real-Time Assistance)

During active calls, Perennia AI can provide real-time suggestions directly to your screen:

- **Talking points** — Relevant mortgage product features
- **Objection handlers** — Scripted responses to common concerns
- **Compliance reminders** — Required disclosures you haven't made yet
- **Rate info** — Current rates for the loan type being discussed
- **Question prompts** — Discovery questions to ask
- **Closing techniques** — How to move toward commitment
- **Warnings** — Compliance or risk alerts

Whispers are delivered within 2 seconds of detecting a trigger moment. They appear as unobtrusive notifications — the borrower never sees or hears them.

## Call Screening

Before a call even reaches you, the system runs multi-tier screening:

1. **Whitelist** — Known contacts connect immediately
2. **CRM Lookup** — Existing leads/clients are identified and their profile is pulled up
3. **Blocklist** — Known spam callers are blocked
4. **Spam Score** — Unknown callers are scored for spam likelihood
5. **Screening** — If truly unknown, the AI receptionist asks for name and purpose before connecting

---

## The Review Queue

Not everything is auto-approved. When the AI isn't confident enough, items land in your review queue:

- **Extractions below 85% confidence** — Review the extracted value against the source transcript
- **Three decision options**: Approve (accept as-is), Reject (discard), or Modify (correct the value)
- **Bulk actions** — Approve or reject multiple items at once
- **Priority sorting** — Lowest confidence items appear first

## How Call Intelligence Links to Your CRM

Everything feeds back into the loan file:

- **Transcripts** are attached as file communications
- **Extracted data** auto-populates lead and loan fields
- **Tasks** appear in your task dashboard
- **Summaries** are searchable across all your calls
- **QA scores** contribute to performance dashboards
- **Compliance scores** feed into audit reports
- **Risk flags** appear on the loan profile

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Use the built-in dialer or AI receptionist** — External calls need manual upload
2. **Review low-confidence extractions daily** — The more you review, the more the system learns your patterns
3. **Check your QA scores weekly** — Identify trends in your call performance
4. **Use follow-up SMS drafts** — They're personalized to each call; review and send within minutes
5. **Don't ignore risk flags** — The underwriter agent catches things you might miss on a busy day
6. **Ensure recording consent** — Say "this call may be recorded" in the first 2 minutes, especially in two-party consent states (CA, FL, IL, MD, MT, NE, NH, PA, WA)

## FAQ

**Q: Do borrowers know the call is being analyzed?**
A: The standard recording disclosure covers this. No additional disclosure is required for AI analysis of calls you're already recording.

**Q: How long does processing take?**
A: Transcription completes within 30-60 seconds. Full analysis (extraction, summary, QA scoring) completes within 2-3 minutes of call end.

**Q: Can I search across all my call transcripts?**
A: Yes. Every word is indexed and searchable. Search by borrower name, topic, date range, or keywords.

**Q: What if the AI extracts something wrong?**
A: Use the review queue to correct it. Low-confidence items are flagged automatically. You can also manually review any extraction.

**Q: Does this work for video calls?**
A: Yes, if audio is captured. Video meeting recordings are processed through the same pipeline.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: All PII is encrypted at rest and in transit. SSNs are stored as last-4 only. Transcripts are redacted before AI processing. All access is audit-logged.
